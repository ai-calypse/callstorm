PRINCIPLES

Four rules that carry the style.

Quiet surfaces

Use the warm canvas for breathing room and white for the area where work happens.

Purposeful emphasis

Dark olive identifies the action. Pastels support categories and context.

Regular typography

Create hierarchy through size and spacing, with limited bold emphasis.

Progressive disclosure

Keep the current decision clear. Open secondary evidence when it is needed.

Strong strokes, quiet fills, readable labels.

chart-efficiency#657F3B

Use this anatomy

  1. A question the chart answers.
  2. Measure, unit, and direction of improvement.
  3. A plain-language takeaway.
  4. A target or meaningful baseline.
  5. Sample size and underlying evidence.

Avoid these shortcuts

  • Unlabeled axes or ambiguous units.
  • Color as the only distinction.
  • Percentile sums presented as stage totals.
  • Missing data silently replaced with zero.
  • Text that becomes tiny on mobile.

BEHAVIOR

A complete system includes how it responds.

Keyboard & assistive technology

  • Give controls real labels and semantic roles.
  • Keep tab order logical and focus visible.
  • Associate errors with the affected fields.
  • Use accessible dialog behavior and restore focus.
  • Provide a control or data table for inspectable charts.

Touch & text scaling

  • Keep body text and controls readable on small screens.
  • Use comfortable 48-unit native controls.
  • Do not disable operating-system text scaling.
  • Reflow content instead of shrinking a desktop screenshot.
  • Pair statuses with words, not color alone.
